1. What are we building?

We are building a multi-tenant AI Knowledge Workspace for organizations. Users can create an organization, invite team members, assign roles, and manage organizational resources. Authorized members can upload documents and use an AI assistant to search, analyze, summarize, and answer questions based on the organization's knowledge.

2. Who will use it?

The primary users are small and medium-sized companies or teams that have accumulated large collections of internal documents and need a faster way to find and understand information from those documents.

3. What problem does it solve?

Important organizational knowledge is often buried inside old documents, reports, manuals, policies, and other files. Employees may spend significant time manually searching through files to find relevant information. Our system allows organizations to preserve their original documents while extracting and organizing their knowledge into a searchable AI-powered knowledge system. Users can ask questions in natural language and receive answers based on relevant documents and sources.

4. What are the three most important features?
A. Organization and team management

Users can create organizations, invite and remove members, and assign roles with different permissions.

B. Document knowledge management

Authorized members can upload documents, monitor their processing status, and make organizational knowledge available for search and AI analysis.

C. AI-powered knowledge assistant

Users can ask questions about organizational documents. The system retrieves relevant information and generates grounded answers with source references while minimizing unsupported answers.
